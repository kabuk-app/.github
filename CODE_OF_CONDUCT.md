# Code of conduct

We want Kabuk to be a place where people can work together without fear of harassment.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful and assume good faith.
- Accept criticism of your work, and give criticism of the work, not the person.
- Help newcomers.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, insults, discrimination or personal attacks.
- Publishing other people's private information.
- Sexualized language or attention that is unwelcome.
- Spam and trolling.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may remove comments, issues and contributions, and may ban people who break these rules.
To report a problem, use a private GitHub security advisory on this repository or contact a maintainer
directly. Reports are handled privately.

This code is adapted from the ideas of the Contributor Covenant, version 2.1.
